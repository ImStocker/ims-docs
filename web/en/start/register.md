# Registration

## Step 1

Go to the [Registration](https://ims.cr5.space/app/sign-up) page.

- Specify the field `Email`, `User name`, `Password`.
- Then `Repeat password` and click `Sign up`.
- It is necessary to put a check mark next to the field "I agree with [rules](https://ims.cr5.space/en/terms) and [privacy policy](https://ims.cr5.space/en/privacy)".
- If desired, you can `subscribe to our newsletter`.

![Registration](../images/register_en.png)

## Step 2

To complete the registration, you must confirm your email by clicking on the link from the email that will be sent to the specified email.

## Step 3

After registration, you can log in at any time. To log in, you need to specify `Email`, `Password` and click `Log in`.

![Log in](../images/login_en.png)
