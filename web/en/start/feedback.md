# Feedback

Do you have any questions, ideas or suggestions? Share them in our [telegram chat](https://t.me/imscreators_en), [Discord](https://discord.gg/p7YeMreDjT) or write to us [here](https://ims.cr5.space/en/feedback ).

Fill in the following fields:
- `Subject` - for example, a partnership offer, a mistake, a payment question, etc.
- `Your Email address for reply`
- `Message` - describe the problem, idea, or suggestion in detail
